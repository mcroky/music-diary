Operator: Roky Studio · Effective October 27, 2026

Diary titles, notes, dates, emotions, music and room settings, drafts, language preferences, earned notes and unlocks stay in local app/browser storage. We do not collect or transmit them to a server. No account, ads, analytics, tracking SDK or microphone collection is used. Music is synthesized on your device without an external music API. Android assets are bundled; the web edition downloads static assets from its hosting provider, which may process connection information under its own policy.

At your request, JSON backups and MP3/WAV files are exported to your chosen destination using Android’s system picker, without broad storage, photo or microphone permissions. A cloud file provider you choose applies its own transmission and retention policy. Records remain until you delete them or clear app/browser data. Uninstalling deletes app records. Android automatic cloud backup and device transfer are disabled. Manage and delete exported files separately. Transfer web diaries by importing JSON; there is no automatic synchronization. Local records are not separately encrypted by the app.

External links open in the system browser and the destination’s policy applies. Feedback you submit is separate from local diaries. For privacy rights or support, use Roky Studio feedback ↗. You do not need to send diary contents.
